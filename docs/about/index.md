## Biography

I am a Machine Learning (Deep Learning) scientist with interests in applications of
computer vision in healthcare and life sciences. I am a Principal Machine Learning
Scientist at [**Genentech**](https://www.gene.com/), which I joined in **January 2025**
when Genentech acquired [**Spring Science**](https://www.springscience.com/) (formerly
**Spring Discovery**); I build single-cell embeddings and foundation models for
high-content imaging and optical pooled screens. At Spring I developed foundation
models for drug imaging data and ML features used by biologists and by several big
pharma partners. Previously, I was at [**PathAI**](https://www.pathai.com/) as a
Manager and Research Lead building state-of-the-art, interpretable multiple-instance
models for digital pathology. I have a Ph.D. in Computer Science from
[**Brandeis University**](http://www.brandeis.edu/) with a specialization in
applications of deep learning in vision and language, an M.A. in Computer Science
from Brandeis, and a B.S. in Biomedical Engineering. In the past, I have worked at
**Microsoft Research**, **Qualcomm Research**, and **Philips Research**.

I am from Kathmandu, [**Nepal**](https://media.gadventures.com/media-server/image_library/Nepal-Himalaya-Mountains-Annapurna-Pokhara-Prayer-Flags-IS-027332084-Lg-RGB.jpg).
Other than research, I love [rock climbing](/notes/climbing/),
[running](https://runkeeper.com/user/kvpaxos/profile), [playing chess](/notes/chess/)
and listening to classical Indian songs.

## Research highlights

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-shield-half:{ .lg .middle } __Adversarial robustness__

    ---

    Defenses against imperceptible adversarial perturbations without retraining:
    pixel deflection + wavelet denoising (CVPR 2018), and an adaptive JPEG encoder
    (DCC 2018). Robust discriminative localization maps (CV-COPS 2018).

-   :material-microscope:{ .lg .middle } __Interpretable ML for pathology__

    ---

    Additive MIL (NeurIPS 2022): exact spatial credit assignment for whole-slide
    models; AI quantification of nuclear morphology predicting genome instability
    and prognosis (npj Precision Oncology 2024).

-   :material-dna:{ .lg .middle } __Foundation models for single-cell biology__

    ---

    Foundation models and single-cell embeddings for drug imaging data at
    Spring/Genentech with 5× gains over off-the-shelf models; PBMC inflammasome
    benchmark datasets and confounder removal (SLAS 2023).

-   :material-brain:{ .lg .middle } __Memory & language networks__

    ---

    Condensed memory networks for clinical diagnostic inferencing (AAAI 2017);
    stacked residual LSTMs for paraphrase generation (COLING 2016); dependent
    reading BiLSTM for NLI (NAACL 2018).
